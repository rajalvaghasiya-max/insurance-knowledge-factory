# Book VII — The Engines of the Knowledge Factory

> *"Departments define responsibility. Engines perform the work."*

---

# Introduction

The Knowledge Factory is organised into departments.

Departments define responsibility.

Engines provide capability.

An Engine is a reusable capability responsible for performing one specialised transformation within the Knowledge Factory.

Engines do not exist independently.

Every Engine belongs to exactly one department.

Every Engine manufactures one or more Knowledge Assets.

Every Engine follows common engineering standards.

Consistency allows the factory to evolve without increasing complexity.

---

# The Role of an Engine

An Engine exists to perform work that can be reused repeatedly throughout the factory.

Engines are intentionally independent of products.

Products consume the results produced by Engines.

They do not own the Engines themselves.

Every Engine should be replaceable without changing the operating model of the factory.

Architecture protects the factory.

Engines implement the architecture.

---

# Engine Design Standard

Every Engine within PolicyScna follows the same specification.

Every Engine defines:

• Mission

• Why It Exists

• Department

• Responsibilities

• Inputs

• Outputs

• Knowledge Assets Manufactured

• Contracts Consumed

• Contracts Produced

• Events Generated

• Dependencies

• Success Metrics

• Failure Modes

• Version

This consistency allows every future contributor to understand an Engine before reading its implementation.

# Engine Evolution

The Knowledge Factory is expected to evolve continuously.

New Engines may be introduced.

Existing Engines may be replaced.

Engines may be combined or divided as understanding improves.

The operating model remains stable because responsibilities belong to departments rather than individual Engines.

Architecture evolves gracefully when capabilities evolve independently.

---

# The Engine Philosophy

Engines are not products.

They are capabilities.

Products are temporary.

Capabilities endure.

Every Engine should therefore be designed for reuse before optimisation.

The value of an Engine is measured not by the amount of work it performs, but by the number of future capabilities it enables.

The strongest Engine is the one that quietly powers many parts of the factory without drawing attention to itself.

That is the philosophy of engineering within PolicyScna.
