# Book VI — The Departments of the Knowledge Factory

> *"A factory becomes exceptional not because it has exceptional machines, but because every department knows its purpose."*

---

# Introduction

The Knowledge Factory is organised into specialised departments.

Each department exists for one reason.

Each department performs one responsibility exceptionally well.

Each department manufactures assets that become the raw material for the next department.

No department works in isolation.

No department duplicates the responsibilities of another.

Together, these departments transform fragmented insurance information into trusted insurance intelligence.

This organisational structure is intentionally independent of programming languages, frameworks or implementation details.

Departments describe responsibilities.

Engines implement them.

---

# Department Operating Principles

Every department within the Knowledge Factory follows the same operating principles.

Every department has:

• A clear mission.

• Clearly defined responsibilities.

• Clearly defined inputs.

• Clearly defined outputs.

• Ownership of specific Knowledge Assets.

• Responsibility for the quality of its outputs.

• Stable contracts with neighbouring departments.

Departments collaborate.

Departments do not overlap.

Departments do not compete.

They strengthen one another.

---

# Department I — Receiving & Inventory

## Mission

Receive every source of insurance evidence entering the factory and establish it as a trusted source of truth.

---

## Why This Department Exists

The factory cannot manufacture trustworthy intelligence if it does not know what evidence exists.

Receiving & Inventory ensures that every document entering the factory is identified, versioned, preserved and registered before any further processing begins.

It protects the integrity of the factory's raw materials.

---

## Manufactures

Evidence Assets

• Evidence Registry

• Evidence Records

• Source Metadata

• Document Versions

• Raw Evidence Archive

---

## Responsibilities

Discover new evidence.

Collect documents.

Monitor changes.

Version evidence.

Register evidence.

Classify document types.

Preserve original sources.

---

## Consumes

External insurance information.

---

## Supplies

Production Control.

---

## Success Measures

No evidence is lost.

Every document is uniquely identifiable.

Every version is preserved.

Every source is traceable.

Production Control ensures that every document follows the correct production line.

---

## Manufactures

Operational Assets.

• Work Orders

• Production Queue

• Production Status

• Factory Events

---

## Responsibilities

Plan work.

Prioritise production.

Monitor progress.

Coordinate departments.

Handle retries.

Record operational events.

---

## Consumes

Evidence Assets.

---

## Supplies

Every production department.

---

## Success Measures

Every document progresses through the factory exactly once.

Production remains observable.

Failures are recoverable.

# The Department Chain

Every department exists because the previous department created the conditions necessary for its success.

Receiving creates trusted evidence.

Production Control creates order.

Document Processing creates structure.

Knowledge Manufacturing creates understanding.

Quality Assurance creates trust.

Knowledge Warehouse creates memory.

Intelligence Products create value.

Together they form the complete operating system of the Knowledge Factory.

No department is more important than another.

The strength of the factory is determined by the strength of every department working together.
