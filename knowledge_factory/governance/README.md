# Factory Governance Cell (FGC)

The Factory Governance Cell preserves the engineering knowledge of the PolicyScna Knowledge Factory.

It ensures that architectural decisions, engineering principles, deferred improvements, lessons learned, and major milestones are permanently recorded and remain searchable throughout the life of the project.

The governance layer consists of five registers:

1. Engineering Principles (FEP)

2. Factory Decision Log (FDL)

3. Factory Evolution Register (FER)

4. Factory Lessons Learned (FLL)

5. Factory Milestones (FMR)

Unlike production departments, Governance does not manufacture customer-facing assets.

It manufactures engineering knowledge.
