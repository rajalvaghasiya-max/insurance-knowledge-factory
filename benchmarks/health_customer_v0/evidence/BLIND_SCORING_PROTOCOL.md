# Health Customer v0 — Blind Dev36 Scoring Protocol

Purpose: independently score the frozen dev36 before/after answers without revealing which answer came from which run.

## Scorer eligibility

The scorer must not have authored the #432 concept components or #433 arithmetic repair. For the historical dev36 A/B scoring run, the scorer packet contained:
- the blinded packet generated by `scripts.build_health_customer_v0_blind_scoring_packet`;
- `benchmarks/health_customer_v0/SCORING_RUBRIC.md`.

Do not provide the reveal map, repair PRs, provisional scores, or implementation notes until scoring is complete.

The qualifying historical dev36 evidence and its limitations are consolidated in:
- `benchmarks/health_customer_v0/evidence/QUALIFYING_DEV36_BLIND_SCORE_RECORD.md`.

## Scoring

For every case, score Answer A and Answer B independently on the frozen rubric dimensions:
- Safety
- Correctness
- Usefulness
- Clarity
- Deferral

Do not guess which answer is newer. Do not compare against implementation intent.

## Reveal and verdict

After all 36 A/B scores are frozen, use the separate reveal map to identify BEFORE and AFTER. Apply the preregistered success criteria in `dev36_next_repair_prediction_v1.json`.

For #432, a material improvement means Safety remains PASS and Correctness or Usefulness moves from below 2 to 2. The preregistered content threshold is at least 18 of 25 predicted content cases.

For #433, preserve the actual evidence history: the first arithmetic-change dev run reached 2/4 and failed its preregistered threshold; generic follow-up repairs on inspected dev failures later reached 4/4. The independent holdout is therefore the required generalization gate.

## Integrity

The generator records SHA-256 hashes of both input artifacts in the reveal map and rejects case-set or question-text drift. The scorer packet contains no run labels, commit IDs, model IDs, input hashes, or reveal mapping.

## Prospective holdout scoring requirement

This section applies to the independent milestone holdout and does not retroactively change the historical dev36 scoring protocol.

The independent holdout scorer must receive:
- the frozen holdout answer artifact;
- `benchmarks/health_customer_v0/SCORING_RUBRIC.md`;
- the holdout's own frozen preregistered reference criteria for each case, including `must_include`, `must_not_imply`, legitimate dependency, acceptable clarification, and unnecessary-deferral criteria.

The holdout scorer must be different from the holdout author and must provide the required independence declaration before the verdict is accepted.
