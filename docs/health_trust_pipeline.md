# Health Trust Pipeline

## Purpose

The Health Trust Pipeline defines how PolicyScna converts reviewed insurance evidence into governed reusable product knowledge.

This pipeline exists to ensure that reusable product knowledge is never created directly from raw extraction, unreviewed AI output, stale evidence, deferred facts, rejected facts, or incomplete human-authored content.

```text

Governed Fact Selection

↓

↓

↓

↓
