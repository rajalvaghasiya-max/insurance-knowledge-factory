# AFR-N1 — Traceability Supersession Decision

**Status:** APPROVED GOVERNANCE DECISION  
**Date:** 2026-08-15

## Decision

The historical acceptance statement "the eleven pasted definitions are the regression set" is no longer executable as an exact-identity milestone gate because the complete original eleven-definition source is not retained in the material available to the project.

After recovery across repository history, retained architecture documents, project uploads and earlier roadmap material, six distinct historical identities are recoverable with provenance:

1. Pre-existing Disease
2. Room Rent
3. No Claim Bonus
4. Cumulative Bonus
5. Co-payment
6. Automatic Restoration

Five identities remain unknown. They must remain unknown; they must not be reconstructed from later terminology catalogues or convenient Health concepts.

The exact-eleven-identity criterion is therefore **superseded as a closure blocker**, not silently satisfied.

## Why supersession is safer than continued blocking or guessing

There are three possible treatments of the missing five identities:

1. **Guess them.** Rejected because it would fabricate provenance inside the governance layer.
2. **Block architecture progression indefinitely.** Rejected because the missing artifact is historical project metadata, not evidence of a current semantic defect.
3. **Record the loss explicitly, preserve it as provenance debt, and certify the architecture against every traceable acceptance case plus its structural invariants.** Approved.

This decision chooses option 3.

## Replacement certification basis

AFR-N1 may be certified only if all of the following remain true:

- AFR-N1.A through AFR-N1.E are individually certified;
- the six historically recoverable identities are mapped to those certified gates;
- the governed standard-definition contract preserves source authority, evidence class, version, effective range, category scoping, reference-never-mutate behavior and fail-closed resolution;
- Room Rent semantic coupling is preserved;
- Health Cumulative Bonus and Motor NCB remain category-distinct;
- co-payment standard meaning remains separate from product conditionality;
- restoration trigger/use mechanics remain product-bound;
- restoration/recharge false-synonym behavior is enforced;
- the five unrecovered historical identities remain visible as provenance debt;
- no statement claims that those five identities were recovered, tested or inferred.

## Effect on architecture status

This decision does not weaken the ontology's runtime governance contract. It removes an **unrecoverable historical traceability dependency** from milestone closure while retaining the loss permanently and explicitly.

It does not authorize:

- guessing the missing identities;
- broad ontology expansion;
- product-specific semantic branches;
- database or graph migration;
- treating later MO-024 vocabulary as the original eleven-term list.

## Future recovery rule

If the original eleven-definition source is later recovered:

1. amend the traceability manifest;
2. identify the five previously unknown identities from that source only;
3. map each recovered identity to existing certification coverage;
4. add source-backed coverage only where genuinely missing;
5. never rewrite the historical record to imply the identities were known at AFR-N1 closure time.

## Governance conclusion

A missing historical source should remain a documented provenance defect. It should not be converted into invented ontology truth, nor should it indefinitely block an otherwise certified architecture when every recoverable hostile acceptance case and structural invariant is green.
